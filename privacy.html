Privacy policy: personal-backup

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Who uses this app

personal-backup is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

The app uses the Google Drive scope drive.file. With this scope it can see and change only the files that it created in the signed-in user's Google Drive. It cannot see any other file.

How the data is used

The app writes encrypted backup files to the owner's Google Drive and reads them back to check or restore a backup. It uses Google user data for no other purpose.

Sharing and storage

The app does not share, sell, or transfer any data to any other person or service. The backups stay in the owner's Google Drive, encrypted. The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
